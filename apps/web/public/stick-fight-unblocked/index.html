The game they usually mean is Stick Fight: The Game by Landfall Games: up to four stick figures fight on small, hazardous maps while weapons rain down from above, and the last one alive wins the round. Play against one to three CPU fighters or grab a friend for two players on one keyboard. It runs in any modern browser on a laptop, Chromebook or phone.

How to play Stick Fight Unblocked

  1. Pick a mode on the start screen: 1 Player against one to three CPU fighters (Easy, Normal or Hard), or 2 Players on one keyboard with up to two extra CPU fighters. Choose first to 3 or first to 5 round wins, then press Fight!
  2. Move with A D or the arrow keys and jump with W, ↑ or Space. Press jump again in the air for a double jump. Wooden and metal ledges are one-way: jump up through them, and press S or ↓ to drop back down.
  3. Fight with J (punch) and K (kick), or F and G. A kick hits harder and lower than a punch; pressing either in mid-air turns into a diving flying kick.
  4. Watch for crates on parachutes. Walk into one with empty hands to grab the weapon inside, then press punch to swing or fire it. When the ammo runs out you throw the empty weapon, which can still bonk someone.
  5. Every fighter has 100 health, and the more damage you have taken, the further hits send you. You are out when your health hits zero, when you fall off the bottom or sides of the arena, or when you touch lava. The last fighter standing scores the round.

Stick Fight Unblocked tips & strategy

  • Ring-outs beat health bars. A fighter on half health flies much further from the same kick, so herd damaged opponents toward an edge instead of trading punches in the middle.
  • Save your double jump. If you get knocked off a ledge, wait until you start falling, then double-jump back toward the nearest platform. Using it early leaves you with nothing when you need it.
  • Grab weapons first, fight second. Even the pistol out-ranges every fist. In a four-way brawl, the player who reaches the first crate usually controls the round.
  • Shotgun recoil is a free dash. Each Scattergun blast pushes you backwards, so fire it toward the arena centre when you are near an edge, never with your back to the void.
  • Rockets and bombs break stone. On Crumble Keep, blast the bridge under an opponent instead of aiming at them directly. Cracked blocks also give way a moment after you stand on them.
  • Don’t stand still on a conveyor. The outer belts in Saw Mill push you toward the drop. Keep moving and jump when the floor saw sparks in its slot.

About Stick Fight: The Game

Stick Fight: The Game came out on Steam on 28 September 2017. It was made by a small team at the Swedish studio Landfall Games, best known for Totally Accurate Battle Simulator. According to Landfall’s press kit, it began as a one-person game jam entry by Wilhelm Nylund built on the wobbly physics system from that game. Four developers then turned it into a full release in about three months. A Nintendo Switch version followed in April 2021.

It is built for 2–4 players, either on one couch or online. Every fighter is a physics-driven stick figure, so punches, kicks and gunshots knock bodies around in unpredictable ways, and many rounds end because somebody is thrown off the map rather than beaten up. The Steam version has 100 built-in levels with hazards and destructible blocks, a level editor and a huge library of player-made maps. It also has tens of thousands of mostly positive user reviews.

Wobble Brawl arenas and weapons

Each round is played on one of five arenas, shuffled so you never get the same one twice in a row:

  • Rooftop Rumble: two city rooftops with a window-washer lift moving up and down in the gap between them.
  • Crumble Keep: a stone bridge between two towers. Explosions and bat swings knock out bridge blocks, and cracked blocks crumble a moment after you step on them.
  • Saw Mill: conveyor belts push you toward the edges, a saw blade sweeps above the top ledge and a second one pops out of the floor (watch for sparks).
  • Magma Pit: the floor is lava and the side platforms bob up and down.
  • Sky Swing: no floor at all, just a cloud island and platforms that swing from side to side.

If a round lasts longer than 45 seconds, sudden death starts and purple goo rises from below, so nobody can hide forever.

Stick Fight Unblocked FAQ

Is there an official Stick Fight Unblocked version?

No. Stick Fight: The Game is a downloadable game by Landfall Games for PC, Mac and Nintendo Switch, with no official browser version. Wobble Brawl on this page is igame9’s own free game in the same style.

Can I play against the computer?

Yes. Choose 1 Player and fight one, two or three CPU fighters on Easy, Normal or Hard. The CPU climbs platforms, picks up weapons, recovers with double jumps and avoids the edges.

How do two players share one keyboard?

Player 1 uses W A S D to move and jump, F to punch and G to kick. Player 2 uses the arrow keys, K to punch and L to kick (Numpad 1 and 2 also work). You can add up to two CPU fighters for a four-way brawl.

Does it work on a phone or Chromebook?

Yes. On touch screens the 1-player mode shows on-screen buttons for moving, jumping, dropping, hitting and kicking. Two-player mode needs a physical keyboard.

How do I pick up a weapon?

Walk into a crate or a dropped weapon while your hands are empty. Press punch to use it. Empty weapons are thrown automatically, and a heavy hit can knock a weapon out of your hands.

Is my progress saved?

Your chosen mode, CPU count, difficulty and match length are saved in your browser, along with your win–loss record against the CPU, your knockouts and your best win streak.
