In this browser game you steer a rolling ball along a track that floats in a dark, neon-lit void. The ball rolls forward on its own; you nudge it left and right, jump over gaps and swerve around spikes and steep drops. One slip and it plummets into the abyss. The appeal is the same as other ball runners like Slope: simple controls, a fast camera and a run that ends in a heartbeat.

On this page you can play Neon Orb Rush, igame9’s own neon ball runner in the same style. It is an endless skyway that curves, rises and falls, narrows to a single tile and splits into lanes, with jump pads, boost strips, sliding blocks and a new colour theme at every stage gate. It runs in any browser on desktop, Chromebook and phone, with nothing to install.

How to play Extreme Run 3D

  1. Press Space or click Roll. The orb starts rolling by itself on a straight stretch, so you have a moment to get the feel of it.
  2. Steer with ← → or A D. On a phone or with a mouse, press anywhere and drag left or right — the further you drag, the harder it turns. The orb keeps its sideways momentum and drifts outward on curves, so steer early and counter-steer through bends.
  3. Jump with Space, ↑ or W, the round ▲ button, a quick tap or an upward flick. Jump over gaps, spike strips, low red bars and the red blocks that slide across the track.
  4. Roll over a yellow pad to be launched across long gaps, and over a pink boost strip for a burst of speed and bonus points. Tall red pillars can’t be jumped — go around them.
  5. Collect gems for points. Your score is metres travelled plus 10 per gem, plus bonuses for boosts and stage gates. Every 500 m you pass a gate into a new stage: the colours change and the track gets faster and trickier.

Extreme Run 3D tips & strategy

  • Steer in taps, not long holds. A short tap moves the orb about one tile. Holding the key builds up sideways speed that is hard to stop on a narrow track.
  • Fight the curve before it fights you. On a left-hand bend the orb slides to the right, and the faster you go, the harder it slides. Lean into the bend a little before it starts.
  • Jump late over short gaps. A jump covers more ground the faster you go. Leave it until the orb is close to the edge, or you may come down short of the far side.
  • Follow the gems. Gem lines always sit on a safe path through holes, spikes and pillars, so they show you where to steer as well as giving points.
  • Don’t jump on a pad. Pads launch you on their own. Pressing jump on the pad is harmless, but jumping just before it can carry you over the pad and into the gap.
  • Land in the middle after a drop. Some gaps end in a lower platform. You have a little time in the air, so line up with the centre of the landing.

About the original Extreme Run 3D

The game was made by AlienWebGames and first appeared on web game portals in December 2023 as an HTML5 game. Portals describe it as a rolling-ball endless runner: you guide a ball along neon pathways, jump to avoid gaps and swerve to avoid spikes and steep drops. Its retro neon look and fast pace invite comparisons with Slope.

The original is split into levels that get harder in steps, from easy to “extreme”. The exact number of levels differs between the sites that host it. Later levels add traps such as rotating platforms, swinging hammers, lasers and platforms that rise and sink. On a keyboard you move with the arrow keys and jump with Space or ↑. Most versions also let you switch the camera view with C and hide the control hints with H.

Every track piece, explained

  • Holes — missing tiles. Steer round single holes and jump across gaps that span the whole track.
  • Spike tiles — red tiles covered in spikes. Touching one while rolling ends the run. Full-width spike strips have to be jumped.
  • Low bars — knee-high red walls across the track. Jump them.
  • Pillars — tall red columns that are too high to jump. Steer between them.
  • Sliders — red blocks that slide from side to side. Time your pass or jump over them.
  • Jump pads — yellow tiles that throw the orb high across long gaps, sometimes onto a lower platform.
  • Boost strips — pink tiles with arrows. A short burst of speed and +25 points.
  • Narrow and split sections — the track shrinks to one or two tiles, or splits into two lanes with a hole in the middle. One lane is always clear.

Stages, speed and orb skins

The orb starts at a gentle pace and speeds up the further you go. Every 500 m you roll through a glowing gate into the next stage. Each gate is worth 100 points and brings a new colour theme: Midnight Grid, Sunset Drive, Toxic Circuit, Ice Highway, Crimson Core and Ultraviolet. The obstacles also get denser over time: early on you mostly meet holes, gaps and bars, while pillars, sliders, zig-zags and split lanes show up later.

Gems you collect are added to a lifetime total that never goes down. It unlocks six orbs: Cyan Core (free), Magenta Pulse (100 gems), Solar Flare (300), Lime Volt (700), Prism (1,500) and Void Pearl (3,000). Orbs only change the look and the light trail; they all handle the same.

Is this the same as Run 3?

No. The names are similar, but Run 3 is a different game. In Run 3 a little alien runs through tunnels in space and can walk onto the walls. In this game a ball rolls on an open track with no walls at all: if you leave the edge, you fall. If you like this one, try Temple Run 3 or Drift Boss next.

Extreme Run 3D FAQ

Who made Extreme Run 3D?

It was developed by AlienWebGames and released as a browser game in December 2023. igame9 is not affiliated with AlienWebGames; the game on this page is igame9’s own ball runner in the same style.

How do you jump in Extreme Run 3D?

In the original you jump with the space bar or the up arrow and steer with the left and right arrows. In Neon Orb Rush you can also use W, a quick tap, an upward flick, or the ▲ button on touch screens.

Can I play it on a phone or Chromebook?

Neon Orb Rush works on phones, tablets and Chromebooks: drag anywhere to steer and tap the ▲ button to jump. It runs in the browser with no download.

Why does the ball slide off on curves?

The orb keeps its momentum, so on a bend it drifts toward the outside, more strongly at high speed. Steer into the bend a little early and the drift is easy to hold.

Is my best score saved?

Yes. Your best score, best distance, lifetime gems and chosen orb are stored in your browser’s local storage on this device. Clearing site data resets them.

What is a good score?

Reaching the first stage gate at 500 m is a solid first goal. Past 1,500 m the orb is fast and pillars, sliders and split lanes appear often, so scores above 3,000 take real control.
