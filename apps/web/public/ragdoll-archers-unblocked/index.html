How to play Ragdoll Archers Unblocked

  1. Choose Gauntlet to fight AI archers one by one, or 2 Players for a duel on one device. Each round starts with a short countdown.
  2. Press and drag back anywhere in the game. The arrow flies the opposite way, and a longer pull means more power. A dotted arc shows the first part of the flight. Release to shoot. On a keyboard, use ↑ ↓ to aim, ← → for power and Space to shoot.
  3. Check the wind box at the top. Wind pushes arrows sideways during the whole flight, and the dotted arc ignores it, so aim into the wind.
  4. Watch the yellow stamina bar. Every shot costs stamina and it refills over a few seconds. A headshot does more than twice the damage of a body hit; arms and legs do less.
  5. Knock the other archer’s health to zero to win. In the Gauntlet you earn coins for the win, each headshot and the health you have left. Spend them on upgrades between rounds.

Ragdoll Archers Unblocked tips & strategy

  • Range in, then go for the head. Your first arrow tells you where the arc lands. Change only one thing at a time, angle or power, until you hit, then raise your aim a little for the head.
  • High arcs beat obstacles. Rocks, pillars and trees in the middle block flat shots. A steeper angle with more power drops arrows over them.
  • Don’t empty your stamina. Three quick misses leave you unable to shoot while the AI keeps firing. One aimed arrow beats three rushed ones.
  • Shoot while they wobble. A hit knocks the opponent off balance for a moment, and a staggered archer’s next shot is less accurate.
  • Buy Sharp Tips and Tough Hide first. More damage and more health help in every round. Splitter arrows are the best early special: three arrows make a hit much more likely.
  • Save specials for later in the fight. Special arrows refill every round, so there is no reason to end a round with any left.

About the original Ragdoll Archers

The game was made by Ericetto, the name used by indie developer Erik Imashev, who also made the ragdoll games Ragdoll Hit and Ragdoll Drop. According to CrazyGames, it launched in the browser in May 2023 and came to Android that September. You control a bow-carrying stickman with ragdoll physics and fight waves of enemy archers. Defeated enemies give skulls, which you spend on health, stamina, damage and new arrows such as electric, poison, balloon and axe arrows. Apples on the field restore health and stamina.

Besides the single-player mode, the original offers two-player co-op and player-versus-player on one keyboard. Its listing gives Player 1 the W A S D keys and left Shift, and Player 2 the arrow keys and right Shift.

Ragdoll Archers Unblocked FAQ

Who made Ragdoll Archers?

The indie developer Ericetto (Erik Imashev). CrazyGames lists a May 2023 web release and an Android version from September 2023. igame9 is not affiliated with Ericetto; Quiver Clash is our own game in the same style.

Is Quiver Clash free, and does it need a download?

It is completely free and runs in the browser on desktops, Chromebooks, tablets and phones. There is nothing to install and no account.

How do I aim in Quiver Clash?

Press anywhere and drag backward, away from your target. The direction sets the angle and the length of the pull sets the power, and a dotted arc previews the start of the flight. Release to shoot. Keyboard players use the arrow keys and Space.

Can two people play on one computer?

Yes. In 2 Players mode, Player 1 uses W/S to aim, A/D for power and F to shoot; Player 2 uses the arrow keys and Enter. On a touch screen each player drags on their own half. The first to win three rounds takes the match.

Why do my arrows miss when the arc looks right?

The dotted arc shows only gravity and the first part of the flight. Wind keeps pushing the arrow sideways all the way, so in strong wind aim higher or lower depending on its direction and strength.

Is my progress saved?

Your coins, upgrades, current Gauntlet round and best round are stored in your browser on that device. Clearing site data or using a private window resets them.
